Operational Safety & Disclosures:
• Live capture is EXPERIMENTAL: Passive capture is restricted to authorized interfaces (defaulting strictly to Windows Npcap loopback 127.0.0.1). No packet injection, port scans, or automated firewall blocking are performed. Live capture is not operationally validated for enterprise defense.
• In-Distribution Split Estimate: Model v2 metrics are an in-distribution estimate from one 80/20 CIC-IDS-2017 split; they do not establish out-of-distribution generalization.
• Unevaluated Minority Classes: Heartbleed, Infiltration, and SQL Injection have zero test support and are unevaluated.
• Baseline Model v1: replacement_v1 is unevaluated by the v2 test partition.
• Uncalibrated Confidence: Model confidence scores are uncalibrated softmax scores and do not establish correctness.
